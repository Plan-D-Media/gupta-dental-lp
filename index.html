Questions people ask before booking

What does the 15% off cover?

How do I get the offer?

Book through this page, call either clinic or message us on WhatsApp. The discount is shown on the written treatment plan you get before anything starts.

Is root canal treatment painful?

The infection hurts. The treatment is done under local anaesthetic, so you feel pressure rather than pain. Most patients are surprised by how ordinary it is. Tell us at the start if you are anxious and we will work slower.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome at both clinics until 9 PM, but you may wait. Booking ahead means you are seen at your slot.

Which clinic should I choose?

Whichever is closer. Both run the same treatments and the same doctors. Phoolbagan is open all seven days; Seven Tanks is closed on Thursday.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We do first visits, fillings, fluoride application and habit correction. Bring them for a look by age three rather than waiting for a complaint.
