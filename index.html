    Answers before you book

    What does the 15% offer include?

    The 15% is taken off the treatment fee for any treatment at either clinic until 31 December 2026. It cannot be combined with any other offer.

    How do I claim the offer?

    Book through this page, call either clinic or send us a WhatsApp message. The discount appears on the written treatment plan you receive before treatment starts.

    Does root canal treatment hurt?

    The pain usually comes from the infection itself. The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. If you feel anxious, tell us at the start and we'll take it slower.

    Can I visit without an appointment?

    Yes. Both clinics accept walk-ins until 9 PM, though you may have to wait. If you book ahead, you're seen at the time you chose.

    Which clinic should I visit?

    Whichever is closer to you. Both offer the same treatments with the same doctors. Kankurgachi is open seven days a week, and Dum Dum Seven Tanks is closed on Thursdays.

    Do you treat children?

    Yes. We provide first dental visits, fillings, fluoride application and habit correction. We recommend a first check-up by your child's first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth appearing, even if there are no complaints.
